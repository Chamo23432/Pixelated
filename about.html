About Pixelated

A small studio building focused software — no bloat, no noise, just tools that work.

Pixelated started as a home for one project — Egypt Academy — and grew into something bigger: a small company built around a simple idea. Software should be built with care, shipped without bloat, and made available in whatever form fits how people actually work.

Every app we release comes in multiple builds — a full version, a stripped-down lite version, and a portable version that runs without installing anything. We build for Windows and Linux using Tauri, keeping our apps small, fast, and native-feeling on both platforms.

We're not trying to be a massive company. We're a small team (for now, just one) shipping tools we'd actually want to use ourselves — starting with education, and expanding into AI, system utilities, and whatever problem catches our attention next.
